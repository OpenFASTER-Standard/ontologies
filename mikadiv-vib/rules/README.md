# MiKaDiv-VIB — Business Rules

Cross-field conditional-mandatory logic not expressible in the XSD alone.
**Status: empty.** Expected to stay thin for VIB specifically -- VIB has
no independent handbook of its own, so most real business rules will live
in `mikadiv-fm/rules/` instead; this directory exists for the rare case a
rule is genuinely VIB-specific (e.g. Clearstream's own collection-format
conventions, not BZSt's).
